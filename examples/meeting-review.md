# Example: Reviewing meeting comments and commitments

This example uses an invented transcript. The meeting date is unspecified.

## Supplied transcript

| Time | Speaker | Recorded words |
|---|---|---|
| 00:40 | Alex | What evidence supports the completion figure? |
| 01:10 | Morgan | I can prepare a sample report by Thursday. |
| 02:05 | Casey | The draft shows 95 percent completion. |
| 02:35 | Alex | Does that count the excluded tasks? |
| 03:15 | Morgan | The current total is 82 completed tasks out of 100 received. |
| 04:00 | Casey | Let's verify the calculation before we send the report. |

## Request to the meeting agent

Apply `institutional-intelligence`, `discovery` and `judgment`. Identify important comments, commitments and observed behaviors. Link findings to the transcript and list questions that need follow up. Check the arithmetic using the numbers in the transcript.

## Example findings

| Finding | Source | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| Alex asks for evidence and checks whether exclusions affect the figure. | Alex at 00:40 and 02:35 | Two observed questions in one discussion. |
| Morgan offers to prepare a sample report by Thursday. | Morgan at 01:10 | An offer with a stated relative date. Acceptance and the calendar date remain unclear. |
| Casey proposes checking the calculation before sending the report. | Casey at 04:00 | A proposed action. An owner and explicit agreement are still needed. |
| The transcript contains a reported rate of 95 percent and counts that give 82 percent. | Casey at 02:05 and Morgan at 03:15 | 82 / 100 = 82 percent. Source records and counting definitions are needed to reconcile the reports. |

## Tentative interpretation

Alex focused on the evidence behind a reported measure during this discussion. The record contains two examples from one meeting. The questions could reflect a reporting responsibility, earlier context or concern about this particular draft. Additional comparable records would help establish a recurring tendency.

## Follow up questions

* Who accepted ownership of checking the calculation?
* Was Morgan's offer accepted, and which calendar date does Thursday refer to?
* Which records and counting rules produced the reported 95 percent?
* Do earlier notes show similar questions from Alex, and what context explains them?

The findings use the supplied transcript and a calculation from its reported numbers. Verification of the underlying task records remains open.
