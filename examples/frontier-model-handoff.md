# Investigating changed output after a conversion

This invented example illustrates a proposed experiment and an unresolved causal claim. The [complete records](complete-handoffs/model-experiment-review-reuse/README.md) carry the evidence through Inquiry, Toolcraft, Judgment and Contribution.

## Inquiry

Claim `C-101` proposes that numerical rounding alone caused changed output. The invented source `E-101` reports changed output alongside different tokenizer settings and runtime versions. Its provenance is documentary, its relationship qualifies the causal claim and its scope covers one prompt and two artifacts. The source leaves several confounders unresolved.

Candidate explanations include rounding, tokenizer settings, runtime behavior and measurement error. A matched environment could help distinguish them.

## Toolcraft

Plan `X-101` proposes matched runtime, tokenizer, prompt, sampling and hardware, plus a negative control and comparison of token identifiers and numerical outputs. Its status is proposed. Execution and results remain unverified. Input evidence `E-101` remains unchanged.

## Judgment

The causal claim remains underdetermined. The plan supplies a next check. Evidence of an executed and valid test is still required to assess its outcome.

## Discovery and Contribution

Discovery could preserve the mismatch as a lead. Intentional interference remains an untested hypothesis. Contribution retains the proposed test design with its limits. The parent investigation remains incomplete.
