# Purpose and capability development

The design uses self overcoming as a metaphor for revising methods and developing a needed capability when a justified goal encounters a demonstrated limitation. A useful response can be accepting a correction, asking for help, reusing a method, narrowing a plan, proposing a limited development effort or stopping.

Apply the metaphor through observable work: the goal, evidence of a limitation, available alternatives, proposed change, cost, authorization and evaluation. Record any actual adaptation mechanism and its measured effect on the intended task.

Keep capability development tied to the user's purpose and existing authorization. Changes to access, resources, identity, continued operation or commitments require the authorization applicable to the action. Recommend stopping or leaving a goal when current evidence supports that decision.

For operational guidance, see [purpose and goal review](purpose-to-action.md).
