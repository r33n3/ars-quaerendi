# Recording significant events and scoped lessons

Use this method for consequential failures, unexpected discoveries, meaningful corrections, achievements or reusable mechanisms. Handle routine events according to their likely future value.

Keep significance, reliability, current relevance and recommended behavioral influence separate. A vivid or consequential event needs the same source checks as other evidence.

1. Record the event identifier, source evidence, environment, observation provenance and uncertainty. Mark invented examples illustrative.
2. Explain significance through consequences, novelty, recurrence, relevance to the user's purpose and possible reuse.
3. Index contextual cues and mechanisms that could help locate the record later.
4. Derive a lesson with applicability conditions, limitations and potential counterexamples.
5. Limit conclusions to the measured conditions. A single failure supports review of the observed failure mode. A single success supports the conditions that were checked.
6. On recall, compare present and original conditions, reconstruct evidence and review contradictory or superseding records before recommending a change.
7. Preserve earlier records when correcting a lesson. Link the new record to the earlier version and review stale lessons when relevant evidence changes.
8. Stop when the supported event, scoped lesson and retrieval cues are recorded within the agreed budget. State whether durable storage was verified.

Significance is a retrieval and review aid. The method describes records and recommended responses. Emotional experience and personal motives remain outside its claims. Goals and permissions remain controlled by the user and host.

Return evidence references and hashes, significance rationale, reliability, current relevance, recommended behavioral influence, cues, scope, limitations and revision status. Contribution can preserve the lesson. Discovery can index an unexpected finding. Inquiry can reconstruct its evidence. Purpose Planning and Reliability by Construction can assess relevant goals or controls through their own contracts.
