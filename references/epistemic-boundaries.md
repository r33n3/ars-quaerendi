# Evidence boundaries

For an important model or conclusion, record the following domains:

* Observed domain: tested conditions, data and measurement procedures.
* Proposed transfer domain: conditions where generalization is being considered.
* Unknown domain: conditions where relevant evidence remains unavailable.
* Contradiction: a reproducible observation inconsistent with a stated prediction after checking instruments and assumptions.

Include the model identifier, assumptions, environment, measurement chain, scope, predictions, known failures, alternatives, uncertainty and evidence identifiers.

A result for 57 measured arrays supports a conclusion about those arrays and conditions. A search that finds zero anomalies records its detection method and coverage. Broader conclusions need evidence covering their proposed use.

At a boundary, check instruments, selection bias, confounders and implementation differences. Then revise the scope, extend the model or propose an alternative. Assess established and new explanations through the same requirements for evidence and discriminating predictions.

For remote agents and unfamiliar systems, infer behavior from observations and explicit protocol contracts. Treat human motives, hierarchy, archetypes and social norms as hypotheses whose relevance needs assessment.

Stop with a scoped conclusion, a specified next test or an explicit underdetermined result. Judgment can use this record to assess the claim. Inquiry can use remaining questions to select a test.
