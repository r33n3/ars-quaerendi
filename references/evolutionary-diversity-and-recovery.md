# Diversity and recovery

Use this reference when several components may share a failure mode or when a recovery result is being interpreted as an improvement.

Alternatives can reduce correlated failures when their dependencies and mechanisms differ in relevant ways. They can also increase maintenance cost and exposed interfaces. Compare actual dependencies, observed failure conditions and resource costs.

Agents or services may differ in model, toolchain, hardware and investigative method. Check which dependencies they share and which failures a difference could prevent. Different names alone leave independence unresolved. Preserve the evidence of a failed component or method.

Recovery restores acceptable service after disruption. Adaptation changes a strategy for new conditions. Repeated selection tests and retains alternatives over successive cycles. Cumulative improvement requires evidence that retained changes improve later outcomes. Assess each claim with the relevant observations.

Biological diversity can suggest questions about alternatives and bottlenecks. Use operational definitions and measured results to assess the system. Variants remain limited experiments serving the user's purpose within actual permissions and resource limits.

Return relevant shared dependencies, candidate alternatives, tested failures, observed recovery, limits and the next useful check. Use [method comparison](adaptive-variation-selection.md) when choosing among variants.
