# Comparing and retaining alternative methods

Use this method when evidence shows that an approach has degraded under changed conditions, a shared failure affects several components or a limited comparison could improve the outcome. Continue a working approach when a separate comparison offers little value.

1. Record the user's purpose, baseline behavior, environmental conditions, observed failure or changed requirement, actual authorization and resource limit.
2. Identify the claim being assessed. Recovery restores acceptable function. Adaptation changes a method to fit conditions. Repeated selection retains tested variants over iterations. Cumulative improvement requires evidence of better outcomes over time. Additional capability needs its own evidence.
3. Propose a small set of useful alternatives with distinct mechanisms. Consider repair, simplification, reuse, authorized collaboration, redesign or a pause. Retain the current baseline as a control when feasible.
4. Record predictions and acceptance criteria before testing. Compare representative tasks under stated conditions, with relevant regressions, failure modes and resource costs.
5. Recommend keeping the baseline, selecting a tested variant, retaining alternatives, holding or stopping. Include the evidence and reasons. Assess benefits through useful outcomes and costs.
6. Preserve provenance, tested scope, uncertainty, contrary evidence, rejected variants and their mechanisms. A selected method retains its operating limits. Preserve the earlier record when revising a conclusion.
7. Revisit the comparison when environmental conditions, model capability or relevant evidence change. A previously unsuccessful method may merit a new test under changed conditions.

## Evidence and records

The [adaptation record](../schemas/adaptation-record.schema.json) links the purpose, baseline, environmental change, predictions, criteria, variants, comparison evidence, recommendation and resource limit. Use shared evidence identifiers, hashes and snapshots. Proposed variants remain labeled proposed until execution evidence exists. A completed comparison records its baseline and variant evidence separately, conditions, criterion assessment, observed outcome and regressions.

Selecting a variant recommends a tested candidate by identifier. Check record consistency and evidence prerequisites. The method still needs to assess measurement validity, representative coverage and whether the evidence supports the recommendation. Human and host controls govern adoption, execution, deployment and commitments.

For correlated failures and recovery claims, consult [diversity and recovery](evolutionary-diversity-and-recovery.md). See the [proposed recovery comparison](../examples/adaptive-peer-recovery.json) for a plan whose results remain unresolved.

## Stopping and consumers

End at sufficient evidence for a scoped recommendation, the agreed budget, unavailable conditions, an instruction to stop or a justified decision that further comparison adds little value. Record useful partial results and open checks.

Purpose Planning can select the proposed change. Reliability by Construction can assess recovery. Discovery can preserve new leads. Judgment can assess the conclusion. Performance and Metric Integrity can check outcomes and costs. Contribution can retain useful methods and rejected alternatives. Apply each skill through its existing contract.
