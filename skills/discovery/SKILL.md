# Discovery

## When to use
Notice anomalies, patterns, weak signals and serendipitous discoveries. Do not invoke for routine tasks already solved by existing tools.

## Method
Record expected versus observed results, raw evidence, environment and provenance. Separate anomaly from interpretation. Test for instrument errors, confounders, coverage and coincidence. Change representation where helpful: bytes, hex, entropy, parsed structure, visualization, numerical patterns or execution traces. Develop alternative hypotheses and cheap discriminating tests. Preserve even out-of-scope leads without claiming novelty or silently changing the parent goal.

## Shared contract
- D documented; O directly observed; R independently reproduced; H hypothesis; U unknown; X contradicted.
- Evidence level is not authorization. Main agent owns purpose, orchestration, permissions, tool execution and P2P A2A trust.
- Do not fabricate measurements, search results or execution. Prefer the smallest informative next action.
- Output scoped evidence, limitations, progress and a justified next step or stop.

See `references/` in the package for extended methods.
