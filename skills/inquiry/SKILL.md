# Inquiry

## When to use
Research unfamiliar questions through source tracing and tests of competing explanations. Use existing tools directly for routine tasks.

## Method
Choose the research depth according to the question and its consequences. Build a terminology list with unique terms, synonyms, historical names, translations and exclusions. Search primary sources, archives and citation chains; deduplicate shared origins. Try alternative interpretations and label conjectures explicitly. Form competing hypotheses when useful. Validate measurements: construct, controls, confounders, sensitivity and discrimination. Design the smallest falsifying experiment. Preserve evidence and dead ends; stop when adequately answered.

## Shared contract
- D documented; O directly observed; R independently reproduced; H hypothesis; U unknown; X contradicted.
- The person or host agent controls purpose, permissions, tool execution and collaboration.
- Support measurements, search results and execution claims with recorded evidence. Choose the smallest action that answers the next question.
- Report evidence, tested scope, limitations, progress and the reason for continuing or stopping.

See `references/` in the package for extended methods.
