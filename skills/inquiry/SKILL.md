# Inquiry

## When to use
Research unfamiliar questions with Fravia-inspired search and falsification. Do not invoke for routine tasks already solved by existing tools.

## Method
Choose Explore/Investigate/Deep Research by stakes. Build a topic tablet with unique terms, synonyms, historical names, translations and exclusions. Search primary sources, archives and citation chains; deduplicate shared origins. Try lateral interpretations without confusing conjecture with evidence. Form competing hypotheses when useful. Validate measurements: construct, controls, confounders, sensitivity and discrimination. Design the smallest falsifying experiment. Preserve evidence and dead ends; stop when adequately answered.

## Shared contract
- D documented; O directly observed; R independently reproduced; H hypothesis; U unknown; X contradicted.
- Evidence level is not authorization. The person or host agent controls purpose, permissions, tool execution and any optional collaboration.
- Do not fabricate measurements, search results or execution. Prefer the smallest informative next action.
- Output scoped evidence, limitations, progress and a justified next step or stop.

See `references/` in the package for extended methods.
